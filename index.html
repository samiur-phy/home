Samiur R. Mir

I am a graduate researcher in high energy physics phenomenology, working at the intersection of theory and experiment.

The Standard Model is the rulebook that tells us how the elementary particles interact in Nature. It has been a very successful model, but incomplete, as it cannot explain neutrino mass and dark matter. As a phenomenologist, I connect theoretical model predictions and real data, complementing our current endeavor to understand the physics beyond the Standard Model. I am also interested in precision measurements from Standard Model calculations, which help us look for new physics signatures, across environments that run from terrestrial detectors to astrophysical laboratories. I am also fascinated by machine learning methods for hypothesis testing, limit setting, and anomaly resolution.
