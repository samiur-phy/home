Samiur R. Mir

I am a particle phenomenologist working at the intersection of theory and experiment, with a focus on neutrinos and dark matter. I am particularly interested in how theoretical models manifest in real data. I want to probe the structure of particle physics through experimental signatures and existing anomalies. My work spans a broad range of environments—from terrestrial detectors to astrophysical laboratories—where I test hypotheses, explore model implications, and study the fundamental phenomena that shape our understanding of the Universe.
