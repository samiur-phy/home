Samiur R. Mir

I am a graduate researcher in high energy physics phenomenology, working at the intersection of theory and experiment.

Standard Model is a the rulebook that tells us how the elementary particles intereact in Nature. It has been a very successful model, but incomplete as they cannot explain neutrino mass and dark matter. As a phenomenologist, I connect theoretical model prediction and real data and complement our current endeavor to understand the physics beyond the Standard Model. I am also interested in precision measurements from the standard calculations, that will help us look for new physics signatures, across environments that run from terrestrial detectors to astrophysical laboratories. Furthermore, I am fascinated in machine learning methods for hypothesis testing, limit setting, and anomaly resolution.
