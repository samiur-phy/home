Samiur R. Mir

I am a graduate researcher in particle phenomenology, working at the intersection of theory and experiment.

My interest is in how theoretical models show up in real data. I look for beyond-Standard-Model signatures in existing measurements and standing anomalies, across environments that run from terrestrial detectors to astrophysical laboratories—testing hypotheses, working out what a model actually implies for an observable, and setting limits where the data allows.
